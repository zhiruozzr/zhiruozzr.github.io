  <h2>Research</h2>
<article class="paper">
    <div class="paper-title">Adaptive Bayesian Shrinkage of High-Dimensional Panel VARs</div>
    <div class="paper-authors">with Firmin Doko Tchatoka and Qazi Haque</div>
    <p class="paper-summary">A Bayesian framework for high-dimensional panel VARs that combines adaptive shrinkage and variable selection, allowing for cross-sectional interdependence and unit-specific heterogeneity. The empirical application studies financial contagion in euro-area sovereign bond markets.</p>
    <div class="paper-links">
      <a href="/files/Adaptive.pdf" target="_blank">Paper</a>
      <a href="/files/Adaptive_Slides.pdf" target="_blank">Slides</a>
    </div>
  </article>
<article class="paper">
    <div class="paper-title">Bayesian Network Estimation for High-Dimensional Panel VARs</div>
    <div class="paper-authors">with Firmin Doko Tchatoka and Qazi Haque</div>
    <p class="paper-summary">A Bayesian Graphical Network Lasso for sparse precision-matrix estimation in high-dimensional PVARX models, with an application to the international transmission of natural-disaster shocks.</p>
  </article>
<article class="paper">
    <div class="paper-title">Disentangling Spillover Networks and Transmission Channels in Panel VARs</div>
    <p class="paper-summary">A bi-level spike-and-slab framework that jointly identifies bilateral spillover links and the variables operating within those links, producing an interpretable global macroeconomic transmission network.</p>
