**Zhiruo Zhang**, **Firmin Doko Tchatoka**, and **Qazi Haque**  

This paper develops a Bayesian framework for estimating high-dimensional panel vector autoregressions (PVARs). We propose a novel approach that combines Bayesian shrinkage with adaptive variable selection to address over-parameterization and sparsity in high-dimensional panels. Using Laplace-based spike-and-slab priors, the method captures both cross-sectional interdependencies and unit-specific heterogeneity, offering a flexible and robust tool for structured inference.

Monte Carlo simulations show that our approach outperforms existing regularization methods in estimation accuracy and forecasting performance. In an empirical application to euro area sovereign bond markets, the model uncovers asymmetric financial contagion and generates stable, reliable multi-country macroeconomic forecasts. The findings highlight the value of adaptive Bayesian shrinkage for modeling heterogeneous and evolving linkages in complex panel data systems.
